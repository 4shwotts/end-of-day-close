Payment reconciliation engine

Reconcile a day's payments in seconds

Upload a bank statement and your internal ledger, and this tool matches them automatically, explains every match, and shows you what still needs checking.

Try a sample day {{ icon("arrow-right") }} {{ icon("upload") }} Upload your own files

How it works

  1. {{ icon("upload") }}

    1 Upload both files

    The bank statement (the money that actually came in or went out) and the ledger (the company's own record of what it sold and refunded).

  2. {{ icon("spark") }}

    2 The engine matches and explains

    It pairs what it can, allowing for fees, late settlement (money arriving a day or two after the sale) and rounding, and says why in plain English.

  3. {{ icon("search") }}

    3 You review only the exceptions

    Anything it cannot explain, such as a missing, duplicated or mistyped entry, is listed with a suggested next step.

File format

UTF-8 CSV with a header row and exactly these columns, in this order. Dates are written year-month-day (2026-11-13). Amounts are plain numbers with no currency symbol or thousands separator, negative for money going out (-15.20). Each file can be up to 2 MB and 20,000 rows.
