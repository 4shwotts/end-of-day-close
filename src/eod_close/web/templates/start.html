Close the day

A puzzle game about reconciliation: the check a finance team runs every evening to prove that the money the bank received matches what the company recorded. Played before? Choose a level.

What you are doing

You are an operations analyst at Copperkettle Homewares Ltd. You get two lists for one business day: the bank statement from Thistledown Bank (what actually arrived) and the company's ledger (what it booked). They never agree exactly. Card processors keep fees, payments arrive a day or two late, and sometimes an entry is missing, duplicated or mistyped. Your job is to pair up the entries that are the same payment and explain the ones that are not.

How to play

  1. Choose a level below and start the day.
  2. Link each bank entry to the ledger entry, or entries, it belongs with: tick them and choose Link selected.
  3. For anything with no partner, choose a reason: missing, duplicate or a likely typo.
  4. Choose Close the day. You get a score, an explanation of every problem in the day, and the engine's answer to compare with yours.

Where the engine comes in

The same day is also solved by a matching engine, the main part of this project. Each level adds harder problems, and the close screen shows where you and the engine agreed and where you differed. The How it works page shows how the engine stays fast on files with thousands of entries.
