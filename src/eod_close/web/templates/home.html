Reconcile a day

Upload a bank statement and your internal ledger, and this tool matches them automatically, explains every match, and shows you what still needs checking.

Try a sample day Upload your own files

The bank statement is the bank's list of money that actually came in or went out. The ledger is the company's own record of what it sold and refunded. On a real day they rarely agree line for line: card processors keep a fee before paying out, money takes a day or two to arrive (this is called settlement), and sometimes an entry is missing, duplicated or mistyped. This tool works through those differences for you and leaves only the ones a person needs to check.

File format

UTF-8 CSV with a header row and exactly these columns, in this order. Dates are written year-month-day (2026-11-13). Amounts are plain numbers with no currency symbol or thousands separator, negative for money going out (-15.20). Each file can be up to 2 MB and 20,000 rows.
