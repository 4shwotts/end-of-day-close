How it works

The engine pairs up a bank statement with your records, explains every pair, and hands a person only the payments it cannot explain.

What it does

    Load the two lists

    The bank statement and your records.

  2. 2

    You check the rest

Why it is fast: race it

Checking every pair gets four times slower each time the day doubles in size.
