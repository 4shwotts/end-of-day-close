Why it is fast: race it

Checking every pair gets four times slower each time the day doubles in size. The engine looks partners up in an index instead, so it only gets about twice as slow. {{ tip("growth", "an index", "Like the index at the back of a book: instead of reading every page, you go straight to the right one.
